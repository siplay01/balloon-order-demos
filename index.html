Six ways to order from Balloon Atelier

Today every order starts in Instagram DM. Each demo below is a working concept for taking orders on a website. Same sets, same look, a different path to the order.

The demos

  • 1. Shop

    Sets by recipient, like the Instagram highlights: for her, for him, girls, boys, baby. Add the name, add to bag, check out.

    Open

  • 2. Personalize

    One product, fully designed by the customer: name, lettering, filling, bouquet colors. The balloon updates as they type.

    Open

  • 3. Gender reveal

    A page for the $180 box. A doctor or friend picks pink or blue by private link, so the parents never see the answer.

    Open

  • 4. Gift finder

    Three questions (who, what moment, budget), then only the sets that fit. Good for gift buyers who do not know what to choose.

    Open

  • 5. Order by DM

    No checkout. The form writes a complete order message, copies it and opens the Instagram chat. Works with the current way of selling.

    Open

  • 6. Build your set

    The customer picks each balloon (jumbo, oversized, chrome, foil number, star) and its color. The bouquet assembles live, with a height and car-fit check.

    Open
